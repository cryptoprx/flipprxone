## 🆕 Creating a New Wallet

### Option 1: SnapTap WebAuth (Recommended)

**Most Secure Method**

1. Click **"Create New Wallet"**
2. Choose **"SnapTap WebAuth"**
3. Enter a wallet name
4. Choose authentication method:
   - **WebAuth/Biometric** - Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello
   - **NFC** - NFC tag (if available)
5. Follow the authentication prompt
6. **IMPORTANT**: Write down your seed phrase
7. Verify your seed phrase
8. Wallet created! 🎉

**Benefits:**
- 🔐 Dual-key encryption
- 📱 Biometric authentication
- 🛡️ Phishing resistant
- ⚡ Fast and secure

### Option 2: Password Protection

**Traditional Method**

1. Click **"Create New Wallet"**
2. Choose **"Password Protection"**
3. Enter a wallet name
4. Create a strong password
5. Confirm password
6. **IMPORTANT**: Write down your seed phrase
7. Verify your seed phrase
8. Wallet created! 🎉

## 📥 Importing an Existing Wallet

### Import via Seed Phrase

**12 or 24 Word Seed Phrase**

1. Click **"Import Wallet"**
2. Select **"Seed Phrase"**
3. Enter your 12 or 24 words
4. Choose authentication method (SnapTap or Password)
5. Complete setup
6. Wallet imported! ✅

### Import via Family Seed

**Classic XRPL Format (starts with 's')**

1. Click **"Import Wallet"**
2. Select **"Family Seed"**
3. Enter your family seed (e.g., sXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX)
4. Choose authentication method
5. Complete setup
6. Wallet imported! ✅

### Import via Xaman Secret Numbers

**8x6 Grid Format**

1. Click **"Import Wallet"**
2. Select **"Xaman Secret Numbers"**
3. Enter your 8 rows of 6 numbers
4. Choose authentication method
5. Complete setup
6. Wallet imported! ✅

**Supported:**
- ✅ ed25519 keys
- ✅ secp256k1 keys

## 🔐 Securing Your Wallet

### Critical: Backup Your Seed Phrase

**Your seed phrase is the ONLY way to recover your wallet!**

✅ **DO:**
- Write it down on paper
- Store in multiple secure locations
- Keep it offline
- Never take photos of it
- Verify you wrote it correctly

❌ **DON'T:**
- Share it with anyone
- Store it digitally (cloud, email, etc.)
- Take screenshots
- Send it via messaging apps
- Store it on your computer

### Enable SnapTap (Recommended)

If you created a password wallet, upgrade to SnapTap:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Click **"Upgrade to SnapTap"**
3. Follow authentication setup
4. Enjoy enhanced security! 🔐

## 💰 Funding Your Wallet

### Get Your Wallet Address

1. Open your wallet
2. Your address is displayed at the top
3. Click the **Copy** icon to copy it

### Receive XRP

**From an Exchange:**
1. Copy your FLIPPRX ONE wallet address
2. Go to your exchange (Coinbase, Binance, etc.)
3. Withdraw XRP to your address
4. Wait for confirmation (usually 3-5 seconds)

**From Another Wallet:**
1. Copy your FLIPPRX ONE wallet address
2. Send XRP from your other wallet
3. Funds arrive in seconds!

### Minimum Balance

**XRPL Reserve Requirements:**
- Base reserve: 1 XRP (locked)
- Per trustline: 0.2 XRP (locked)
- These reserves are still yours, just locked

**Example:**
- New wallet: Need minimum 1 XRP
- With 1 trustline: Need minimum 1.2 XRP
- With 5 trustlines: Need minimum 2 XRP

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### Can't See My Balance?

- Click the refresh button
- Check your internet connection
- Verify you're on the correct network
- Clear browser cache

### Transaction Failed?

**Common reasons:**
- Insufficient balance (including reserves)
- Invalid destination address
- Missing trustline
- Network congestion

### Forgot Password?

**With Seed Phrase:**
1. Create new wallet
2. Import using seed phrase
3. Set new password

**Without Seed Phrase:**
- Unfortunately, funds cannot be recovered
- This is the nature of self-custodial wallets

### SnapTap Not Working?

- Ensure browser supports WebAuth
- Check NFC is enabled (if using NFC)
- Try biometric authentication instead
- Update your browser
- Use password as fallback
