# Getting Started with FLIPPRX ONE Wallet (fONE)

Welcome to FLIPPRX ONE! Establishing your high-security profile takes less than 60 seconds.

---

## 🚀 Step 1: Open the App

Because fONE is a Single Page Application (SPA), there is nothing to download.
Visit **[one.flipprx.xyz](https://one.flipprx.xyz)** on your Desktop, Tablet, or Mobile browser.

---

## 🆕 Step 2: Establish a Secure Local Profile

When you first load the page, you have two primary options:

### Option A: The SnapTap WebAuth Route (Highly Recommended)

We heavily advise using SnapTap. This binds your wallet to your device's biometric security (FaceID/TouchID/Windows Hello).

1. Click **"Create New Wallet"**
2. Choose **"SnapTap WebAuth"**
3. Your browser will prompt you to authenticate using your device's native security prompt.
4. Scan your face, or enter your device's secure PIN.
5. Setup Complete! The underlying cryptographic keys are now sealed to your hardware. 

### Option B: Import an Existing Wallet

If you are migrating from an older wallet (like Xaman):

1. Click **"Import Wallet"**
2. Depending on your key format, select **"Seed Phrase"** or **"Family Seed (s...)"**.
3. Carefully input the data.
4. You will then be prompted to secure this import using SnapTap Biometrics, ensuring the imported keys are locked down locally.

---

## 💰 Step 3: Funding and Initialization

If you generated a brand new XRPL wallet, your address currently does not exist on the global ledger. 

1. Look near the top of your dashboard to locate your `r...` address.
2. Copy it.
3. Send **at least 1 XRP** to this address from an exchange (like Coinbase, Binance) or another wallet.
4. The moment the ledger clears the transaction (typically 3 seconds), your dashboard will spring to life, showing your live balance and activating all fApps.

---

## 🌍 Step 4: Exploring the fApps

You are now in full control of a multi-chain node. 

Click over to the **Apps** tab located on your bottom or side navigation bar. From here, you can instantly:
- Open the **FLIPPRX Marketplace** to collect native NFTs.
- Use **Address Book** to save your friends' payout addresses.
- Write a few **Encrypted Notes** to safely store sensitive real-life data.
- Switch to **Solana** or **Bitcoin** easily from the top-right dropdown toggle to manage entirely different blockchain infrastructure from the same exact UI.

Welcome to the future of DeFi.
